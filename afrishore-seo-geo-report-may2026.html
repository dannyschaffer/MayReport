Why Google sessions dipped — and why it doesn't concern us

The Google drop is almost entirely in branded searches ("afrishore bpo", "afrishore vacancies") which spike when there are active job postings and dip when there aren't. Non-branded commercial traffic — the kind that converts into B2B enquiries — is not meaningfully down. The work we're doing targets that commercial segment specifically, and it takes 3–6 months to fully register in rankings.

Outbound — LinkedIn

A More Human Outreach Approach

We've moved away from scripted pitch sequences to a genuinely conversational approach: openers that ask specific questions about what the prospect is actually dealing with in their operations. No pitch in the first message — or the second. The prospect opens up, the pain surfaces naturally, and Afrishore enters only when there's an obvious fit. Two recent examples:

Norma Davis — Healthcare Admin, US
"I am interested in Afrishore" → email address provided
Richard Norton — Service Director, Avensys UK
Email address provided → call with Anton confirmed
The shift is deliberate: the old approach opened with what Afrishore does; the new approach opens with what the prospect is actually dealing with. Each opener references specifics from their industry — patient access workflows, HTM compliance documentation, backend capacity — that signal genuine familiarity rather than a mass sequence. When the right pain is there, the prospect identifies themselves. Both conversations above moved to email within a week.
